Desmond Ong
The OnlyKiens Victim
"TBF I was doing fine until I join OnlyKiens." — Des Ong, 25 May 2026, completely serious.

Here's the thing: Des had 6.5 GW wins, the most in the league. He won GW28 on his birthday. He smashed GW31. He called himself "I.sak at FPL" and then watched Isak go to Liverpool while Newcastle collapsed around him. He ranted in the chat about Howe's substitutions. He wanted to "smash the TV controller on tv." Classic Des.

But the transfers? "+1 net." After 9 months, the moves added exactly one point. The GWs were won on raw instinct. The transfers were an elaborate way of breaking even. Ended the season +$27.50 profit — the FPL equivalent of tipping Kien a kopi for the advice that ruined him.

Kien Lam
FPLKienny, Secretary, Treasurer, Content Creator
Every single gameweek, without fail: "GW1: Des Ong, GW2: MC, GW3: Janoe…" Posted religiously. Formatted consistently. All season. The man was secretary, unofficial statistician, watch party organiser, and FPL advisor — then complained when he was made treasurer on top of it all. "Why am I treasurer. I tot I secretary."

Meanwhile, "OnlyKiens" launched mid-season. Nicky credited the channel for his GW27 win. Des blamed it for his downfall. MC got shadow-banned. Kaveh said the endorsements were a "cautionary tale." The channel's most prominent subscriber finished 5th.

Kien ended 3rd with the highest team value in the league (£105m), a top-100k OR, and $132 profit. "I work 9 months for this?" Yes, Kien. Yes you did. Like and subscribe.
